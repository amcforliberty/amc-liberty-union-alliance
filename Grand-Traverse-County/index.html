TRAVERSE CITY RECORD-EAGLE — OCTOBER 9, 2026

Flock Cameras Land on GT Board's Radar

Published Investigative Feature | Print Edition Page 5A | Reporting by Aly Kleidon

Following organized civic turnouts, independent constitutional advocates entered the Grand Traverse County Board session to challenge automated license plate readers and data oversight loops.

"Private technology vendors bypass this commission's centralized legislative oversight by executing fragmented township contracts to rig Traverse City with an intrusive, au-to-surveillance wall," Chell told the board.
"These independent township cameras do not operate in a vacuum; they rely on an un-voted backdoor subsidy from this county, leveraging local county-funded dispatch, servers and terminals to process private alerts, all while Grand Traverse taxpayers pay the hidden operational costs with our hard-earned tax dollars and our liberties," he added.
Political candidates Dr. Mitch Distin and Kate Gallup were among those who spoke out against the cameras, citing similar use of Flock cameras taking place with the Antrim County Board of Commissioners.
