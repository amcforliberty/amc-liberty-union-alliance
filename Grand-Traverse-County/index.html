Breaking Administrative Inertia

Oct 7 Spoken Testimony Transcript Filed: October 7, 2026 | Full Record Text (PDF)

"The reason I am standing here today instead of at my job working is to address a critical vulnerability in how Grand Traverse public assets and administrative labor are being utilized. Private technology vendors bypassed this Commission's centralized legislative oversight by executing fragmented township contracts to ring Traverse City with an intrusive automated surveillance wall... When I and other local workers pass through intersections like South Airport and Barlow, just to earn a living, we are forced to be tracked by technology we never voted on... Proposed Action 1 does not dictate deputy behavior; it directs the county's civilian IT Department to permanently scrub all proprietary vendor access keys and data tokens from county-owned devices."
