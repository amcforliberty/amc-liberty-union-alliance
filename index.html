Declaration of Intent

The AMC Liberty & Union Alliance is established as a non-partisan, independent forum for jurisdictional oversight, public record preservation, and constitutional education.

Our mission rests upon a singular, foundational truth: Our fundamental human rights do not originate from ink on parchment. They are inherent, natural, and born within us. The supreme compacts of our history—the Declaration of Independence, the United States Constitution, and the Bill of Rights—do not grant us liberty. Instead, they serve as defensive barriers, engineered to safeguard our natural-born rights against centralized overreach.

However, paper boundaries are inherently fragile. They possess no automatic enforcement mechanisms. They remain holding lines only so long as the Sovereign People actively hold, defend, and enforce the original intent of those texts. To lose sight of this intent is to lose liberty itself. It is our solemn civic duty to stand as the living guardians of our heritage.

To fulfill this obligation, the Alliance operates across three distinct operational tracks:

  • Historical & Statutory Foundations: Executing deep-dive analyses into constitutional history, foundational principles, and federal frameworks to track where centralized mandates override local autonomy.
  • National Legislative Tracking: Monitoring macro-level legislation—such as international procurement acts and sweeping federal statutory changes—to trace how macro-level policies quietly compromise sovereign interests.
  • Local Jurisdictional Action: Deploying direct public oversight within our regional township and county board chambers. By compiling localized field dossiers, filing precise public record metrics, and issuing technical brief challenges on local soil, we return the power of enforcement to the hands of regional citizens.
Augustus Melvin Chell — Founder, The AMC Liberty & Union Alliance
