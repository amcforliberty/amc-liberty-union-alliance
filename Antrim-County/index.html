"A narrative is circulating that because the Antrim County Board of Commissioners has never formally voted to establish a countywide contract with Flock Safety, our taxpayers remain completely unentangled... That narrative is a dangerous misunderstanding of how this shared database operates. The hard truth is that Antrim County is already compromised. Currently there are already eight active Flock cameras deployed on the roadways of Antrim County—including a camera sitting in an Eastport commercial lot tracking residents on M-88—and three more moving through the pipeline... Pull the plug on Flock Safety, stand up for liberty, and let’s focus our tax dollars on fixing our physical infrastructure."
